# Legal

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This page brings together the agreements and policies that apply when you use services from Wular Data Lake Private Limited ("WularData", "we", "us" or "our") or visit wulardata.com. Please read them before you place an order. By ordering, activating or using any WularData service, you agree to the documents that apply to that service.

## Our legal documents

- [**Customer Agreement**](/legal/customer-agreement) — the master contract between you and WularData for all paid services: accounts, fees, suspension, termination, liability and dispute resolution.
- [**Service Terms**](/legal/service-terms) — additional terms for specific services, including VPS and cloud servers, dedicated servers, colocation, Forex VPS, domains, email, backup and managed IT services.
- [**Service Level Agreement**](/legal/sla) — our uptime and support commitments and the service credits you can claim if we miss them.
- [**Acceptable Use Policy**](/legal/acceptable-use) — what you may not do with our services or network.
- [**Privacy Policy**](/legal/privacy) — how we collect, use, store and protect personal data, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
- [**Cookie Policy**](/legal/cookies) — how wulardata.com uses cookies and similar technologies.
- [**Refund & Cancellation Policy**](/legal/refund-policy) — when refunds apply and how to cancel a service.
- [**Website Terms of Use**](/legal/website-terms) — the terms for browsing and using wulardata.com.

## Which document applies first

If two documents conflict, they apply in this order: (1) a signed order form, quotation or contract specific to your service; (2) the Service Terms, for the service concerned; (3) the Service Level Agreement; (4) the Customer Agreement; (5) the other policies listed above.
