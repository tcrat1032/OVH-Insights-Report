# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Wular Data Lake Private Limited ("WularData", "we" or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on wulardata.com and the WularData Customer Portal. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include pixels, local storage and scripts that collect information about how you use a site. Some cookies are set by us ("first-party cookies") and others by third-party services we use ("third-party cookies").

## 2. How we use cookies

| Category | Purpose | Can you turn it off? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly necessary | Keep the website and Customer Portal working: page loading, security, load balancing, login sessions and remembering your cookie choices | No — the site will not work properly without them |
| Preferences | Remember choices such as language, region or display settings | Yes |
| Analytics | Help us understand how visitors use the site — pages visited, time on page, errors — so we can improve it. Data is aggregated | Yes |
| Marketing | Measure the effectiveness of our campaigns and show relevant WularData content on other websites | Yes |


## 3. Information collected through cookies

Depending on the category, cookies may collect:

- your IP address and internet service provider;
- device type, browser, operating system and approximate location (city or region);
- pages you visit, links you click, the referring website and the time and duration of your visit; and
- login and security tokens for the Customer Portal.

## 4. How long cookies last

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period, which for our cookies is no longer than 13 months, or until you delete them.

## 5. Managing cookies

When you first visit our website, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies through our cookie banner, and change your choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the website footer. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings; your browser's help section explains how. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the website or Customer Portal from working.

## 6. Third-party cookies

Third parties that provide analytics or embedded content on our site may set their own cookies, subject to their own privacy policies. We do not allow them to use these cookies for purposes other than the services they provide to us.

## 7. Changes and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy when we change the technologies we use. The latest version is available at [wulardata.com/legal/cookies](/legal/cookies). For questions, contact our Grievance Officer at [grievance.wdlc@wulardata.com](mailto:grievance.wdlc@wulardata.com).
